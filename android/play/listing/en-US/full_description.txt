Domovoi is an open-source voice assistant for your home that runs on your own hardware. Your voice, your music and your household's data stay with you: no account, no cloud service, no analytics and no ads.

This app is the companion to a Domovoi server: a computer in your home running the free Domovoi software, with small speakers in each room. It also works on its own, with no server at all.

ON ITS OWN, WITH NO SERVER
• An assistant that answers on your phone. Type or tap the mic and ask; a language model running on the phone writes the answer and reads it aloud. After a one-time model download it works in airplane mode, and what you ask never leaves the phone. Needs a 64-bit phone with 8 GB of memory or more and about 3 GB of free space.
• Play the music and videos on your phone, and browse its photos and the folders you choose.

WITH YOUR DOMOVOI SERVER
• Music in every room: browse your library, build a queue and play it in any room.
• Radio stations, podcasts and audiobooks your server provides.
• Chat with your home assistant by text or voice.
• See the whole house at a glance: which rooms are playing, every timer counting down, and anything that needs attention.
• Timer and reminder alerts on your phone when one goes off anywhere in the house.
• Drop in on a room like an intercom.
• Turn a spare phone or tablet into a room: room mode listens for the wake word, answers questions, plays music and rings timers, like any Domovoi room speaker. You turn it on yourself, after a screen that explains exactly what it does; a notification stays while it listens.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• The app talks to your own Domovoi server on your home network, and to nothing run by us.
• The phone's assistant processes your voice on the phone and never stores audio.
• Plain connections are allowed only to addresses on your home network; anything on the internet must use HTTPS.
• Your household pairing is sealed with a key in the phone's keystore and left out of cloud backups.
• No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.

If one of the assistant's answers is offensive or wrong, long-press it to report it: you see exactly what the report holds before anything is sent.

Domovoi is free and open source under the MIT license. Setting up a server, the source code and the documentation are at github.com/coders-farm-official/domovoi.
