Your house, from anywhere. Still yours.

Domovoi is built to stay on your network — its own documentation tells you not to port-forward it. LTL Remote is the way around that without giving anything up: your server dials out to us, and we carry bytes we can't read.

No holes in your router

The Domovoi plugin opens one outbound connection and keeps it open. Nothing listens on your home network. It works behind CGNAT and double NAT, which is most residential internet.

We relay, we don't read

Your phone and your house agree on session keys directly. We forward sealed frames between two sockets and count the bytes for billing. We can see that you connected. We cannot see your dashboard, your library, or your voice.

You approve devices, not us

A device registered here can't reach anything until an admin approves it on your own Domovoi dashboard, after comparing a fingerprint. If we were compromised tomorrow, we still couldn't let anyone in.

How it goes

1 · Install the plugin
Install ltl_remote on your Domovoi server and enable it.
2 · Type eight words
Your dashboard shows a pairing code. Enter it here while signed in. Only a hash of it ever reaches us.
3 · Approve your phone
Add a device here, then approve it on the dashboard — checking that the two fingerprints match.
4 · That's it
Your dashboard, your library, your rooms — from a train.

What we can and can't see

We hold: your email, which households you've paired, their public keys and fingerprints, which devices you registered, when links open and close, roughly where from, and how many bytes moved. That's what routing and billing need.

We don't hold: request paths, headers, bodies, media, or audio. Not because we delete them — because the relay never parses them, so there's nothing to keep. The protocol and the honest list of residual risks are both written down.
