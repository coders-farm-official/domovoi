Domovoi for iPhone and iPad is here.

- Music, movies, audiobooks, radio and podcasts from your own Domovoi server
- Play music in any room of the house, or on the phone
- Save audiobooks to the phone and pick up where you left off
- Your household's files and documents, with editing
- Timer and reminder alerts from anywhere in the house
- Drop in on a room like an intercom
- Turn a spare iPhone into a room
- Works on its own too: your phone's music, videos and saved audiobooks
