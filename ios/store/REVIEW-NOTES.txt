WHAT DOMOVOI IS
Domovoi is the phone app for Domovoi, free, open-source (MIT) software that a household runs on its own computer at home, like the app for a home NAS. Made by Coders Farm, a nonprofit. Source and setup: github.com/coders-farm-official/domovoi

WHAT WORKS WITH NO SERVER (testable now)
On first launch the app opens to the phone's own Music, Videos and Files tabs. Play music and videos from Photos or from a folder you pick in Files. Saved audiobooks appear once one has been saved. This version has no on-device chat: Chat appears only when paired with a household server.

PAIRING, AND WHY YOU CANNOT REACH A SERVER
Everything else (rooms, the library, timers, the intercom) comes from the household's own server on its home Wi-Fi. The app finds it on the local network (the Local Network prompt) or takes a typed address, and the household's admin approves the phone on the server's dashboard. Home servers live on private addresses inside a home, so a reviewer cannot reach one. Plain HTTP is allowed only to local addresses and to .home.arpa, .lan, .internal and .home, names that resolve only inside a home network; anything on the internet must use HTTPS. The attached video, filmed on an iPhone, shows pairing, Home, music in a room, an audiobook saved and played offline, a timer alert, chat and room mode.

ROOM MODE
A spare iPhone can be a room: it scans a room code, the household approves it on the dashboard, and it answers the wake word. It listens only while Domovoi is on screen. The audio background mode is used only for playback and drop-ins (2.5.4); the microphone is never started from the background. We suggest Guided Access to keep the phone in the app. Leaving room mode takes Face ID or the passcode. The wake-word and voice-detection models come from the household's own server, as data (2.5.2).

DOWNLOADS
The app downloads no executable code and no AI model. Room mode's wake-word and voice-detection models are data from the household's own server.

NO HIDDEN FEATURES (2.5.19)
There are no developer options, no hidden gestures and no secret settings. The version line in Settings is plain text. Test switches exist only in debug builds and are compiled out of this one.

NO ACCOUNTS (5.1.1)
The app creates no account, with us or anyone. A phone is paired with a household's own server; that pairing is kept in the Keychain on this device only, never synced to iCloud.

NO THIRD-PARTY AI (5.1.2(i))
Nothing goes to a third-party AI. Chat is answered only by the household's own server, running a model on the household's own hardware. Any reply can be reported from its menu: the person sees the whole report, and it goes by their own email to info@codersfarm.org.

PRIVACY
No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs. Data goes only to the household's own server, which Coders Farm never sees.

IPAD
