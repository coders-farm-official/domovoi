# Domovoi 1.2.0

**The home computer system that just works. Keeps it in the house.**

Domovoi turns a computer you already own into your home's own system: your
music, movies, audiobooks, files and documents in one place, playing in every
room and on your phone. No cloud, no subscription, and nothing leaves your
network unless you say so.

This release follows 1.1.0. Music keeps going: Up next follows a song or a
list with songs like it, in the browser, in the rooms and on the phone, and a
room's queue can be searched, jumped, reordered and saved. Music gains a
Genres tab and a player that opens over any page; Domovoi keeps a listening
history you control. The house also gets shared lists you can fill by voice,
podcast episodes with their details, and a server you can reach as
`domovoi.local`. If this is your first look, start with the
[README](https://github.com/coders-farm-official/domovoi#readme).

## What is new since 1.1.0

### Up next keeps the music going

- **In the browser:** a song played from a list (a playlist, an artist, an
  album, a genre, search results) goes on with the rest of that list,
  leaning toward songs like the ones you let play; a song played on its own
  goes on with similar songs from the library; a whole list played or
  shuffled on purpose plays as it is, and similar songs join when it is
  nearly over.
- **In the rooms:** the same, from "play in kitchen" on a song, an artist's
  or a genre's room buttons, a cast, or a spoken "play <song>" / "play
  something". The server adds the picks to the room's own queue as it
  plays. A cast hands its Up next to the room, and casting back brings it
  home.
- **Each pick says why** ("same artist", "90s", "from road trip",
  "wildcard"), with **less like this** and remove. The **autoplay** switch
  (the player's, or each room's) turns it off. With two people or more,
  **for** picks whose taste the mix follows.
- **A short list no longer repeats itself:** two search results or a
  10-song album play once, then similar songs follow ("more like <list>").
  A list of 50 songs or more still goes round again, reshuffled.
- **After a streamed song** (one you don't have, played through a streaming
  plugin), the room carries on with songs from your library, leaning to
  that artist when you have them.

### A room's queue

- **Music → Room queue has a search box:** type a song, artist, album or
  genre and press **play next** or **add to queue**. Guests get it on a
  room that is playing.
- **Tap any song to play it now**, move songs, take some out, and **save as
  playlist** to keep the queue as a new playlist.
- **A playlist played in a room is the room's whole queue,** in order or
  shuffled; the first song starts at once and a long playlist fills in
  behind it. Changing the queue never changes the saved playlist. A room
  now plays a playlist from its first song, and songs the player can't find
  are skipped ("play the X playlist" says how many).
- **"Add <song> after this"** puts the song in the room's queue right after
  the one playing; it no longer touches any playlist.
- **The tab is quicker,** mostly on phones: a long queue shows 100 songs at
  a time with **show more**, and the room you looked at last loads first.
- **Settings → Rooms → a room** shows the room's next three picks under now
  playing.

### Music

- **A Genres tab** (`#music/genres`): the genres your files' tags name, each
  with its songs and play, shuffle and add to queue, in a room or in this
  browser. After the update the server reads the genre and year of every
  song already in the library in the background; the tab says "reading
  genres from your files… N of M" until it is done. Nothing leaves the
  house for this.
- **A genre and a year from MusicBrainz** for a song whose tags have none
  but which song recognition matched: the recording id only, paced, and
  only while song recognition is on, the Internet answer is not **No** and
  the server is online. A genre in the file always wins.
- **The player opens from the player bar, over any page,** on a computer
  and on a phone: click the cover or title, the up chevron, or **open
  player**. Music no longer has a Player tab; old `#music/player` links open
  it over Music. Under the controls is **Lyrics · Equalizer · Queue ·
  Hide**.
- **Add to the queue from the queue:** the player's Queue ends in a search
  box (**Enter** adds, **Shift+Enter** plays next). **Save as playlist** is
  a smaller button under it.
- **A playlist plays in this browser:** **play here** and **shuffle here**
  in the playlist dialog, Favorites too.
- **"Play Lil Seeto, the song Glitch Again"** now asks for that song by that
  artist.

### Also in Music

- **The room cards on the Music page start folded away** behind **Show
  rooms**, on the dashboard and in the app.
- **Stop and close:** the player bar's **×** stops playback (a room's too)
  and removes the bar. A podcast or audiobook keeps its place.
- **A MilkDrop visualizer** in the expanded player, drawn behind its panel,
  with full screen that keeps the screen on. Twelve presets of Domovoi's own
  come with it; an admin can add `.milk` or Butterchurn 3 `.json` presets.
- **The right artist.** "Play Let E.M. Know by T.I." no longer plays
  another artist's song with a similar title: it streams through a media
  plugin when one is installed, or offers "I have one by Bryson Tiller.
  Want that instead?"
- **A forgiving search** that matches every word, whatever the case,
  accents or punctuation, and **Music → Artists** on the dashboard.
- **Room players that stopped start again** by themselves, for example
  after an update restarted a Windows install's Linux system.

### Song recognition

Recognising songs by their sound (AcoustID, then Shazam) no longer renames
a song to an unrelated one: an answer is used only when it agrees with the
title or artist the track already has, from its tags or its file name. A
track no answer agrees with keeps its names. Untagged files named
"Title - Artist" are read that way round when the artist is already in
your library.

### Lists

- **The house now has shared lists.** **To-do** and **Shopping** are there
  after the update, and you can make your own. Every voice and every paired
  device sees the same lists.
- **By voice:** "add milk to the shopping list", "what's on my to-do list",
  "cross milk off the shopping list", "clear the shopping list" (it asks
  first). "Add eggs to the shopping" works without the word "list". Lists
  never take a playlist or the music queue.
- **On the dashboard:** a **Lists** page under **Tools**; in the Android
  app, a **Lists** screen under Tools, after Calendar.

### Listening history and "this is me"

- **Domovoi now keeps a listening history:** each library song a room,
  browser or phone plays, how long it played, how it ended and who it was
  for. Up next learns from it. It is kept **1 year** by default; an admin
  sets 90 days or **Don't keep**, or deletes it all, in Settings → Privacy →
  **Listening history**. Forgetting a person deletes theirs.
- **"This is me"** on a person's page (Settings → Household → People) marks
  the phone or browser in your hand as theirs: what it plays counts as
  theirs, and they can clear their own history from it. The first device to
  claim a person needs no password; a second one needs an admin. A shared
  screen can't be anyone's. A device marked as someone's before this update
  says "this is me" again once.
- Room queues no longer show the adding device's id to anyone but an admin.

### The Android app

- **Up next in a room's queue:** tap a song to jump to it, **play next** or
  **add to queue**, the picks with why each was chosen, the room's
  **autoplay** switch, **less like this**, and **save as playlist**. A
  playlist played in a room becomes the room's queue.
- **Search to add** at every queue, the phone's and a room's: the full
  player's Queue tab, Music's queue tabs, and a room queue sheet one tap
  away from Home's room rows and Music's room cards. Drag to reorder (move
  up / move down with a screen reader). A long room queue shows 100 songs
  at a time.
- **Music → Genres,** with play, shuffle and queue here or in a room, and
  the full player's **Lyrics · Queue · Hide** tabs.
- **A playlist plays on the phone:** **play here** and **shuffle here** in
  the playlist drawer, Favorites too.
- **Podcast episodes show their details:** when published, how long, how
  far you are, downloaded or not, chapters and the description; the show's
  header says how many episodes and when it was updated; **poll now** names
  the shows that failed.
- **"This is me"** on a person's page for this phone, and the phone sends
  its listening history (kept on the phone until the server has it).
- **Lists** under Tools, after Calendar: add, cross off and put back, edit
  in place, rename, delete, clear the crossed-off items, and reorder by
  dragging. It updates live, and it can go on the phone bar.
- **The media notification:** music, audiobooks, podcast episodes and
  stations each show right in the notification and on the lock screen;
  books and episodes get 15 seconds back and 30 seconds forward; a station
  has no seek bar.
- **A phone used as a room** lets a drop-in's announcement finish before
  the call opens, and waits after a turn that went wrong before waking
  again, as a room device does.

### Your name and your voice

- **"My name is …" spells the name back** and you can correct it ("no,
  it's Jordyn", "it's spelled J-O-R-D-Y-N"). A name that sounds like
  someone the house knows gets "Did you mean Jordan?"
- **Admins can see how each room turn's voice was matched** ("voice: Alex
  0.82 (next: Priya 0.61)") in the Conversations tabs. Only new turns have
  it.
- **A voice is not a name:** switching voices keeps Domovoi's name, and a
  request it has no command for gets "I can't do that yet" instead of a
  made-up "done".

### Wake words

- **Train on synthetic voices only,** and hear the training voices before
  training.
- **Pushing a word to a room sets that room's Wake sensitivity,** shown in
  its Settings tab, instead of a hidden value of the word's own.

### Settings and the room drawer

- **Saving a setting that only a restart applies** (the AcoustID key, a
  speech model, some internet answers) asks **Restart now** or **Later**.
- **A room's drawer:** Health and the command recordings switch are on its
  **Settings** tab.
- **Settings → Household → Media folders** replaces Paths. On a Windows
  install it opens a folder in File Explorer and an admin can move the
  media folders to another drive.
- **The network name** is in **Settings → System → Advanced**; the SD card
  box's download caches fold under "details".

### domovoi.local

- **The server answers to `domovoi.local`,** so a browser on the same
  network can open `http://domovoi.local:6369` (`domovoi2.local` when
  another device has the name). On Windows, once this release's Windows
  side is in, and never while Windows marks the network Public.
- **Rooms** try that name too, and still check the server's key wherever a
  name leads.

### Drop-in

- **A drop-in no longer cuts off "Dropping in on the kitchen."** The room
  finishes the sentence before the call opens. This needs the new room
  software too: upgrade each room.

### Podcasts

- **Episodes show their details:** when each was published, how long it is,
  how far you are in it ("24 min left", "played"), whether it is
  downloaded, its chapters and the first lines of its description. The top
  of a show shows its description, episode count and last check.
- **Big feeds are read** (over 8 MB: the 200 newest episodes), a feed that
  hasn't changed isn't downloaded again, and **Poll now** says which shows
  couldn't be read and why.
- **On a Windows install, episodes get their own `Podcasts` folder**
  (by default `C:\Users\Public\Domovoi\Podcasts`); the next Setup or
  update moves them in, checked file by file.
- The Files page refuses to delete or move the music folder's podcasts and
  audiobooks folders, with the reason.

## Known limits

- **The Android app has no Artists tab yet.** On a phone, a song the
  library has only by another artist is answered "I couldn't find … in
  your library."
- **The visualizer needs WebGL 2**; a browser without it keeps the bars and
  says so. While casting to a room there is nothing to draw. A `.milk`
  preset's custom shaders aren't converted, so it may look plainer
  than in MilkDrop, and Butterchurn's older JavaScript format is refused.
  Keeping the screen on was measured in Chrome and Edge.
- **Up next follows library songs only.** A stream, a podcast or an
  audiobook ends a room's Up next, and a room's Up next ends when the room
  is stopped, cleared, or left paused or stopped for two hours.
- **The MusicBrainz genre and year** need song recognition on and the
  server online; songs without a recognised recording stay in the **no
  genre** row.
- **A name saved misspelled before this release stays as it was:** rename
  the person in Settings → Household → People.
- **`domovoi.local` is a convenience, not proof** that you reached your own
  Domovoi.
- **The Windows installer and the Windows room Setup are not code-signed.**
  Windows SmartScreen will ask before running them.
- **No encryption on your home network.** Domovoi uses plain HTTP inside the
  house and has one shared household key, plus one admin password. It is not
  meant to be reachable from the internet, so do not port-forward its ports.
  The [security notes](https://github.com/coders-farm-official/domovoi/blob/main/docs/SECURITY_PRIVACY.md)
  say plainly what it does and does not guard.

## Upgrading

- **Linux servers:** Settings, System, About & updates, **Update Domovoi**.
  The update applies the eight database changes by itself (the search, the
  synthetic-only wake words, the lists, the voice matches, each track's
  genre and year, the listening history, where a genre came from, and Up
  next) and rolls back by itself if the new code does not come up healthy.
  On a large music library the search and the genre-and-year changes take
  a moment, and the server then reads every song's genre and year in the
  background (a few minutes for a few thousand songs, most of an hour or
  more for 100,000). There is nothing to run by hand.
- **Listening history** is on, kept for a year, from the update. To keep
  less or none, see Settings → Privacy → **Listening history**.
- **Linux with a firewall:** if `ufw` is on, let `domovoi.local` through
  from your network:
  `sudo ufw allow from 192.168.0.0/16 to any port 5353 proto udp`
  ([the network name](https://github.com/coders-farm-official/domovoi/blob/main/docs/LINUX_HOST.md)).
- **Windows installs:** use the dashboard's **Update Domovoi** (Settings,
  System, About & updates). It updates both halves. The Windows side brings
  `domovoi.local` (Setup adds one firewall rule, `Domovoi-mDNS-5353`, for the
  local network only; the uninstaller removes it), the Podcasts folder and
  the Media folders page's open and move.
- **Rooms:** a room takes the new wake-sensitivity handling, the
  `domovoi.local` fallback, the wake log lines and the drop-in that
  finishes its sentence with **Upgrade room** on the dashboard. A room
  keeps waking exactly as it did: it moves the value it was using into its
  Settings tab.
- **Songs renamed by an earlier recognition run:** an admin runs the
  repair once, `POST /api/music/library/enrich/repair` with the admin
  token. It runs in the background without the network, puts back the
  file's own title and artist where the match disagreed, and never touches
  a track you edited by hand.
- **Names:** if someone's name was saved misspelled, rename them in
  Settings → Household → People.
- **Plugin authors:** a plugin page whose route is `lists` is now refused,
  like any core page's route.
- **Scripts:** `POST /api/podcasts/poll` adds `failed` and `failures` (the
  four counts are unchanged), and the lists are at `/api/lists`.
  `POST /api/music/queue/{room}/add` now puts `end` (the default) before the
  room's Up next picks; `next` on a room's Up next session answers `409`;
  the core's `GET /v1/admin/snapshot` no longer carries `current_playlist`.
  New: `/api/music/up-next`, `/api/music/listens`, `/api/music/genres`,
  `POST /api/music/queue/{room}/play` and `…/save-playlist`
  ([API reference](https://github.com/coders-farm-official/domovoi/blob/main/docs/API_REFERENCE.md)).
- **The Music Player tab is gone:** `#music/player` links open the player
  over Music.
- **Android:** install or update the app from Google Play. A phone can also get
  it from your own server: press **Download the app to this server** on the
  dashboard once, then scan the QR code from the phone.

- `domovoi-1.2.0-arm64-v8a.apk` and `domovoi-1.2.0-armeabi-v7a.apk`: the
  Android app, for 64-bit and 32-bit phones.
