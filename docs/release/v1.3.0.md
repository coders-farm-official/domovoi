# Domovoi 1.3.0

**The home computer system that just works. Keeps it in the house.**

Domovoi turns a computer you already own into your home's own system: your
music, movies, audiobooks, files and documents in one place, playing in every
room and on your phone. No cloud, no subscription, and nothing leaves your
network unless you say so.

This release follows 1.2.1. It brings your other calendars home, keeps the
music going in every room (and in several rooms at once), learns who is
speaking far better, and installs on Linux with one command. The Android
app is updated with it. **After updating, everyone in the house says "my
name is ..." once more** (see Upgrading). If this is your first look, start
with the [README](https://github.com/coders-farm-official/domovoi#readme).

## What is new since 1.2.1

### Your calendars, on the household calendar

- **Google, Outlook or iCloud, by link.** An admin pastes a calendar's
  private link in Settings, Household, Calendars, and its events show on
  the Calendar page, on Home and in "what's on my calendar today?". Domovoi
  reads them; it never changes them, and never signs in to anyone's
  account. The link stays on your server and is never shown again.
- **Or straight from a phone.** In the Android app, **Phone calendars**
  ("Share this phone's calendars with the house", off until you turn it on)
  sends the calendars you pick to your server over your own network. No
  internet needed. Turn it off and they are deleted from the server; a
  phone that goes quiet for a week has its calendars removed by itself.
- **Home's calendar card is now today and next:** what is still on today,
  then the week's next events, from every calendar. A private event a
  phone marks as busy is shown and read aloud as busy time.

### Up next keeps the music going

- **The queue everywhere.** Every song has a **...** menu with **play
  next** and **add to queue**. The Queue panel shows this browser's queue
  or any room's, and Home's room cards have a **queue** button.
- **By voice:** "what's next", "play *song* next", "add *song* to the
  queue", "clear the queue", "skip this artist", "turn autoplay off", "why
  is this playing?" and "play something like this".
- **A Music mix** (Settings, Household, Music mix): slide between
  **Discovery** and **Favorites**, **Stay close** and **Wander**, choose
  what happens when a playlist ends, and how long before a song can repeat.
  Each person can have their own sliders. Up next now leans toward songs
  you haven't heard in a while, and tells you why it picked each one.
- **Similar artists from ListenBrainz, if you want them.** Two optional
  switches let Up next bring in artists similar to the ones you have, and
  let an artist's well-known songs come up a little more often. Only
  public MusicBrainz catalogue numbers are sent, never names or what you
  played, and choosing a song never waits on the internet. They follow
  your internet answer, and you can turn either off.

### Groups: one queue, several rooms

- **"Play jazz everywhere"**, "play this in the office too", "join the
  kitchen", "leave the group". Make named groups (say, **Upstairs**) in
  Settings, Rooms, Groups, then "play jazz upstairs".
- In a group, skip, back and queue changes are the whole group's, and so
  are pause and stop. **"Stop here"** stops only the room you are in; the
  others play on. Home shows which rooms are playing together.
- Volume stays per room. Rooms start within a fraction of a second of each
  other, so two speakers heard from one spot can sound a little out of
  step. Podcasts and audiobooks still play in one room.

### A new voice ID

- **It knows who is talking about twice as often.** Domovoi now uses
  3D-Speaker's ERes2Net model. In our simulated house, under the same
  "almost never name the wrong person" rule, it named the speaker in 66 %
  of commands in a home office (was 62 %) and 51 % in a kitchen (was 14 %),
  and it now works across a dining room and even in a garage.
- **Smaller and lighter.** The update removes torch (about 550 MB) from a
  Linux server; the new model is a 25 MB file.
- **Clearer answers.** "Who am I?", "What's my name?" and "Where am I?"
  work however you ask them, and a request too short to tell who is
  talking says so instead of claiming not to know you. The People page
  shows which rooms have learned each voice and where a person was last
  heard.

### Your music library, tidied

- **Music, Tools** (admins): find **duplicates** and move the extra copies
  to a trash you can restore from; review songs that recognition wasn't
  sure about and **Apply** the right match; and get **cover art from the
  Cover Art Archive**, written into MP3 and FLAC files, with the file's
  own pictures saved first and **Undo** to put them back.
- A song that carries its own MusicBrainz ids is used as it is, every
  credited artist is searchable, and `.m3u` playlists in your music folder
  appear as playlists.

### Easier to install

- **One command on Linux:**
  `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/coders-farm-official/domovoi/stable/scripts/linux/install.sh | sudo bash`.
  It asks whether the server will have internet and its time zone, checks
  that what it downloaded is this signed release, installs everything, and
  ends with the address to open from your phone.
- **More Linux systems:** Debian 13, Linux Mint 22 and Pop!_OS 24.04,
  besides Ubuntu 26.04. A fresh install on Ubuntu and Debian is now tested
  every week, with an update and a rollback.
- **The phone app is downloaded at install,** so the server can hand it to
  phones even with no internet later. If it couldn't be, the server says so
  until it is there.
- **New installs wake on "Domovoi"**, Coders Farm's own wake word, trained
  on synthetic voices only. Rooms you already have keep their word; each
  room now shows which word it wakes on, and Settings, Rooms says plainly
  why a room couldn't join.

### Release files you can check

- **Build provenance for every file.** The release is built and signed by
  GitHub Actions behind the owner's approval, and GitHub records where and
  how each file was built (see Verifying a release, below).
- **Software bills of materials** (SBOMs) list the third-party code the
  server, the dashboard and the app ship, for any vulnerability scanner.
- **Reproducible Android builds.** The APKs are built in a pinned Linux
  image, so the same source gives the same bytes; F-Droid can check them.
- **Our security score is public:** the OpenSSF Scorecard badge in the
  README.

## Security fixes

- **Pillow 13.0.0** (pictures, thumbnails, cover art): fixes 43 published
  advisories, five critical, about crafted image files that could crash
  the server, use up its memory or read memory they shouldn't.
- **certifi 2026.10.23**: the trusted certificate list no longer trusts
  the emSign roots whose intermediate keys were compromised (critical).
- **filelock 4.2.10** (Linux server): 13 advisories about lock files a
  local user could abuse.
- **Five fixes from GitHub's code scanning**, each with a test: a crafted
  "yes" reply could freeze the server for days; a crafted document or chat
  draft could stall the dashboard (two fixes); a plugin's table names
  reached the database unquoted in the uninstall dialog; and the join page
  no longer copies a crafted room code into its "open the app" link.
- **The server's signatures now use the `cryptography` library** (OpenSSL)
  instead of Domovoi's own code, which wasn't constant-time. Same keys,
  same signatures: every room keeps trusting its server.

The full advisory list is in the
[release notes](https://github.com/coders-farm-official/domovoi/blob/v1.3.0/docs/RELEASE_NOTES.md).

## Upgrading

- **Linux servers:** Settings, System, About & updates, **Update Domovoi**.
  The database changes (eight of them) run by themselves, and the update
  rolls back if the new code doesn't come up healthy. After it, your music
  library's tags are read once more in the background.
- **Windows installs:** the same **Update Domovoi** on the Version card
  does the Linux part first, then asks for the Windows part, which comes
  from the 1.3.0 Setup. Finish it from the Version card or from the tray
  icon's **Finish updating Domovoi...**.
- **Then, everyone says "my name is ..."** once, in any room. Domovoi asks
  them to spell their name and count to ten, so it learns the voice from a
  few seconds of speech, not one short clip. Afterwards a room that hasn't
  learned a voice yet may ask once, "was that you?"; answering yes teaches
  that room. Old voice fingerprints can't be used by the new model, and Domovoi
  never kept recordings, so until each person does, Domovoi answers
  everyone but names nobody. The People page shows who is left. Anyone who
  had asked Domovoi never to save their voice should say so again.
- **Voice ID settings:** if your `domovoi/.env` sets
  `VOICE_PROFILE_MATCH_THRESHOLD`, `VOICE_PROFILE_SAME_ROOM_MARGIN` or
  `THIRD_PARTY_CLUSTER_MERGE_THRESHOLD`, delete those lines: they are on
  the old model's scale.
- **Internet set to No:** the voice ID model isn't downloaded; copy it in
  by hand ([internet guide](https://github.com/coders-farm-official/domovoi/blob/v1.3.0/docs/INTERNET.md)).
- **Android:** update the app (Google Play, or from your server) for
  Phone calendars, groups and the Music mix.
- **Rooms:** a room whose wake word reads "unknown until this room
  updates" needs **Upgrade room**.
- **Scripts and other clients:** see the
  [API reference](https://github.com/coders-farm-official/domovoi/blob/v1.3.0/docs/API_REFERENCE.md);
  the calendar events answer now includes events from other calendars, and
  now-playing and queue answers carry a `group`.

## Verifying a release

Every file here is listed in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Windows Setup is signed
with Domovoi's release key. New in 1.3.0, each file also has GitHub build
provenance. With the [GitHub CLI](https://cli.github.com/):

```bash
gh attestation verify DomovoiSetup.exe -R coders-farm-official/domovoi
```

It passes only for a file with exactly the bytes the release workflow
published. [SECURITY.md, Verifying a release](https://github.com/coders-farm-official/domovoi/blob/v1.3.0/SECURITY.md#verifying-a-release)
has the details, including how to hold it to the release workflow on
`main`.

## Known limits

- **Voice ID starts from scratch:** nobody is named by voice until they
  introduce themselves again (Upgrading, above).
- **Groups are "separate rooms" grade:** two speakers you can hear from one
  spot may sound slightly out of step, and podcasts and audiobooks don't
  play in groups.
- **Calendars are read-only.** Domovoi shows other calendars but can't add
  to or change them, and it doesn't sign in to calendar services.
- **The Windows installer and the Windows room Setup are not code-signed.**
  Windows SmartScreen will ask before running them.
- **No encryption on your home network.** Domovoi uses plain HTTP inside the
  house and has one shared household key, plus one admin password. It is not
  meant to be reachable from the internet, so do not port-forward its ports.
  The [security notes](https://github.com/coders-farm-official/domovoi/blob/main/docs/SECURITY_PRIVACY.md)
  say plainly what it does and does not guard.
- **The iPhone app is not out yet.**
