# Domovoi 1.0.0

**The home computer system that just works. Keeps it in the house.**

Domovoi turns a computer you already own into your home's own system: your
music, movies, audiobooks, files and documents in one place, playing in every
room and on your phone. No cloud, no subscription, and nothing leaves your
network unless you say so.

This is the first full release. Three test releases (1.0.0-rc1, rc2 and rc3)
came before it. If you tried one of those, this page tells you what is new
since rc3. If this is your first look, start with the
[README](https://github.com/coders-farm-official/domovoi#readme).

## What is new since rc3

### One Chat, on the house or on your phone

- The Android app has one Chat now. It lists the chats on your home server
  (shared with the household) and the ones that stay on your phone, each marked
  with where it runs. Away from home it shows the phone's own.
- Chats name themselves, and you can rename, pin, archive and search them.
- A phone chat can answer about the phone itself (what is playing, the
  battery, free space, Wi-Fi), set a timer or alarm in the Clock app after you
  say yes, start a call, text or email for you to finish, and look at a photo.
  It runs on the phone, in airplane mode too, on a 64-bit phone with 8 GB of
  memory or more. This is still labelled a preview.
- Chat can look things up, but only with your permission. A card shows the
  exact words and where they will go: allow once, allow in this chat, or not
  now. A house that answered "No" to the internet question never sends one.
- Ask Domovoi about Domovoi: the built-in help desk answers from the user
  manual, and says so when the manual does not cover your question.
- AI chat and questions are optional. Setup asks, and Settings turns them off
  or on later. With them off, voice commands for music, timers and the rest
  keep working.

### Documents in chat

- Attach a PDF, Word, OpenDocument, Excel, CSV or text file and ask what it
  says. Answers name the pages they came from, and say "I couldn't find that in
  the document" instead of guessing.
- Long documents can be summarized. Before a request Domovoi is known to be
  weak at, you get an honest heads-up instead of a shaky answer.
- Ask for a resume, a cover letter or a letter. Domovoi drafts it, you ask for
  changes ("shorter", "more formal"), and you save it as a PDF or Word file.
- Photograph a letter or a bill and ask "what does this say?" You get who it is
  from, what it is about, what to do, and the dates and amounts quoted from the
  letter itself.
- In any room, say "read me the lease" and the room reads a document from
  Documents aloud. "Take a letter" turns what you dictate into a document.

### Printing and scanning

- Domovoi prints to network printers and scans from network scanners with no
  drivers, if they work with AirPrint, IPP Everywhere or Mopria (scanners over
  AirPrint's scanning, "AirScan"). Settings, Rooms & devices, Printers &
  scanners finds them, prints a test page and shows paper and ink.
- By voice: "print two copies of the lease" (it tells you the pages and asks
  first), "how much ink is left", "scan this to Documents".

### New Documents and Sheets editors

- **Documents** now open in a word processor in the page: headings, lists,
  checklists, tables, links, colours, fonts and sizes. It saves by itself.
- **Photos** can be pasted or dragged in, resized, captioned, cropped and
  rotated.
- **Fonts:** 73 open-licence font families come with Domovoi and work with no
  internet. An admin can add more for the whole house, which needs the internet
  setting to allow it.
- **Co-editing and history:** several household devices can edit one document
  at once and see each other's names and cursors. A phone's offline edits join
  in when it reconnects. History keeps versions you can open and restore.
- **Word, Markdown and PDF:** download as any of the three. Word files open in
  the same editor.
- **Sheets** open in a new spreadsheet editor that keeps formulas, formatting,
  several sheets and frozen panes, saving into the same `.xlsx` file.
- The Android app opens documents and sheets in the same editors. On a phone,
  a tap anywhere on a document starts typing, and in a sheet a finger scrolls,
  a second tap on a cell edits it, and a long press then drag selects.

### Files

- **Other:** the shared Documents folder now shows only documents, sheets, PDFs
  and drawings. Everything else that was in it (installers, archives, pictures)
  is under a new **Other** view. Nothing was moved or deleted.
- **Upload a whole folder:** the Upload button has Files and Folder. A folder
  keeps its subfolders, shows progress with Pause and Cancel, and asks once
  when some files already exist. Audiobooks of many gigabytes upload without
  trouble.
- **On a phone:** each file row is its name, with its actions in one menu, and
  Documents has a New button. The app downloads a file itself into
  Downloads/Domovoi instead of handing it to the browser.
- **The app keeps only the server part of a typed address,** so one pasted from
  the browser's address bar (with a page after it) saves as that server.

### Rooms

- **Setting up a room** works with more routers. A Raspberry Pi room joins mesh
  systems and WPA2/WPA3 mixed routers, says in words why a join failed, and
  follows its router if the router's security changes later. Prepare the SD
  card again to get this on a new room.
- **A phone as a room warns before its battery runs out.** Off its charger, at
  20 % and again at 10 %, the dashboard and the app on the household's phones
  say so, and the room says it aloud. Each room can turn the spoken and the
  notified warning off.
- **Quiet hours:** Domovoi does not speak announcements (a low battery, a
  printer problem) in a room between 22:00 and 07:00 by default. Alarms, timers
  and reminders still ring, and answers and intercom still speak. Set it for the
  house or for each room. **Every server gets quiet hours on upgrade**, so a
  room that used to speak a printer problem at night no longer does; turn it off
  in Settings, Advanced, Voice & speech to keep the old behaviour.
- Rooms are called **rooms** everywhere you read it now.

### Spare laptops as rooms

- A spare Windows laptop or PC becomes a room with a double-click Setup,
  `DomovoiSatelliteSetup.exe`, made from a code on the dashboard. A spare Linux
  laptop or mini PC still joins with one command, and an old computer can be
  turned into a room from a USB stick.

### One button updates a Windows install

- On a Windows install, **Update Domovoi** now updates both halves: the server
  inside, and the Windows side (the tray icon and helper). The Windows part is
  downloaded from the release, checked against Domovoi's own release key, and
  installed after one administrator prompt on the PC. The card says plainly if
  something went wrong, with a Try again.

### Container guard fixes

- A repair by Setup, an update, or a brand new room no longer sets off the
  container guard over a rebuilt music player, which used to pause updates. A
  rebuilt image that the update itself made is accepted. One built by hand is
  still reported.

### The security work

- The new editors, co-editing and printing each had a written security review,
  and what it found is fixed.
- Windows updates only run a Setup signed with Domovoi's own release key.
- Your own search address must use `https://`, except on your home network.
- The journal never holds the household token, and what people say shows up
  there only as a word count unless you turn on debug logging.
- Domovoi still assumes your home network is your household. It is not meant to
  be reachable from the internet, so do not port-forward its ports. The
  [security notes](https://github.com/coders-farm-official/domovoi/blob/main/docs/SECURITY_PRIVACY.md)
  say plainly what it does and does not guard.

### Also

- Settings is five groups with a search box and one Health page.
- A rewritten user manual, in the dashboard and the app.
- The Android app is about 15 MB smaller per phone.

## Known limits

- **Sheets have no live co-editing.** A sheet says who else has it open, and if
  two people save, the second one chooses to keep theirs or load the other
  version. Documents co-edit; sheets do not.
- **Workbooks with charts, pictures or pivot tables open read-only**, so saving
  can never strip them. A sheet shows its first 5,000 rows and 100 columns; the
  rest of the file is kept as it is.
- **Word files with complex layouts may shift** when saved as Word. You can
  save as a Document beside the original instead.
- **iPhone isn't available yet.** The app is for Android 8.0 or later. An
  iPhone app is its own track.
- **Chat on the phone** needs a 64-bit phone with 8 GB or more of memory and
  about 2.6 GB of downloads. It is a preview.
- **Printing:** network printers only. USB printers and printers that need a
  maker's driver are not supported.
- **Letters and photos:** reading real handwriting is not measured, and reading
  a letter can take about two minutes on a computer with no graphics card. A
  blurry photo may not be readable.
- **The Windows installer and the Windows room Setup are not code-signed.**
  Windows SmartScreen will ask before running them. They remain previews.
- **A USB stick that turns an old PC into a room** has been tried in virtual
  machines; real computers are next.
- **No encryption on your home network.** Domovoi uses plain HTTP inside the
  house and has one shared household key, plus one admin password. See the
  security notes above.
- **The default wake word is "hey jarvis".** Training your own "Hey Domovoi"
  works from the dashboard; a shipped default needs more permissive training
  data.

## Upgrading

- **Linux servers:** Settings, System, About & updates, **Update Domovoi**.
  The update applies the database changes by itself (there are ten since rc3)
  and rolls back by itself if the new code does not come up healthy. New
  installs follow the `stable` channel, which now points at this release.
- **Windows installs:** the same **Update Domovoi** button. After this release
  it also updates the Windows side. An install whose tray icon is from
  1.0.0-rc3 or earlier needs one manual step first: run the new
  `DomovoiSetup.exe` from this release once over the install. From then on the
  button does both halves.
- **Rooms:** Raspberry Pi rooms get their software from the server. Prepare an
  SD card again to get the new Wi-Fi setup on a room that is not set up yet.
  A phone room needs the new app for the faster battery reports.
- **Android:** install or update the app from Google Play. A phone can also get
  it from your own server: press **Download the app to this server** on the
  dashboard once, then scan the QR code from the phone.
